# Research

My research focuses on **functional identities in rings and algebras**. I am interested in how identities involving maps constrain the structure of a ring, and how these ideas extend to more general algebraic settings.

## Research interests

- Functional identities
- Ring theory
- Noncommutative algebra

## Foundational reading

The following books form part of my preparation in this area:

- **K. I. Beidar, W. S. Martindale III, and A. V. Mikhalev**, *Rings with Generalized Identities*.
- **Matej Brešar**, *Introduction to Noncommutative Algebra*.
- **Matej Brešar, Mikhail A. Chebotar, and Wallace S. Martindale III**, *Functional Identities*.
